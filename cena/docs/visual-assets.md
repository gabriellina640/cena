# Elementos visuais

O tema usa rosa claro, texto em rosa escuro e figurinhas de cachorros sem corações ou acessórios.

## Figurinhas

- Arquivo usado pela aplicação: `public/assets/dogs.png`.
- Conteúdo: salsichinha arlequim e pug, com fundo transparente.
- Geração: ferramenta integrada de imagens, sem uso de CLI ou chave de API.
- O arquivo é reutilizado no resumo, no menu e nas telas sem títulos.

### Prompt de geração

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent dog sticker artwork for a light pink personal movie catalog website. Create ONLY TWO cute full-body pet dogs sitting side by side: on the left a silver and chocolate dapple dachshund (salsicha arlequim, long low body, very short legs, soft gray and dark brown marbled coat, long floppy ears); on the right a fawn pug with a chocolate dark muzzle, face wrinkles and a curled tail. The dachshund looks toward the viewer with a gently tilted head, the pug looks cheerful, friendly and relaxed. Premium hand-drawn sticker illustration with soft watercolor-like pastel shading, warm dark brown outlines, clean thick white die-cut outer border, natural gray-brown and beige dogs. Composition landscape, both entire dogs clearly visible and separate with a little space between them, centered with transparent margin. The dogs should be lovely and readable at small website sizes. NOTHING ELSE: no hearts, no heart shapes, no accessories, no clothes, no sunglasses, no bows, no flowers, no popcorn, no sofa, no objects, no text, no scenery, no watermark. Truly transparent background with alpha, not a solid background and no checkerboard drawn into the image.
